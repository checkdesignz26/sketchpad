🖌️ Create custom brush
Try it out
Taper length is a percentage of the stroke. A tapered monoline is drawn as a solid brush.
An image tip (a PNG with a transparent background works best) is stamped along the path, like your Motif Brushes.
Grain texture
Any picture works — light areas let the colour through, dark areas hold it back. Greyscale textures give the cleanest result.
Blending
The brush blends with whatever is already on the same layer. Colours can look different on a transparent layer.
Colour variation
Each mark along the stroke shifts slightly from your chosen colour. Not available for a uniform monoline; picture tips need “Colour with current colour” switched on.
Dual brush
Only the parts of the stroke that overlap the second tip are kept, which gives speckled, bristly or stippled marks.
